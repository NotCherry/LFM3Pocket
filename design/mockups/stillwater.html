How do I keep a sourdough starter alive for nine days if there’s no fridge when I land?

LFM3

Keep it stiff, cool, and unfed until you’re back.

A pourable starter peaks and falls within a day. Before you leave:

  1. Feed 1:2:2 so the paste is thick, not runny.
  2. Let it rise about halfway, then seal the jar.
  3. Pack it on a cold pack. Under 15°C if you can.

At home, discard most of it and feed twice. A rise means it lived. No rise and a nail-polish smell means start over.
